## Introduction

This is a really simple and versatile cheery sauce recipe that I've used for macaroni, in a pie or tart and just about anywhere you'd want to use a cheesy style sauce

## Equipment

* Weighing scales
* Small saucepan / milk pan
* Spatula or wooden spoon
* Teaspoon
* Tablespoon
* Mug

## Method

1. Put the kettle on, put the stock cube in a mug, put the mug on the scales, zero and pour 100ml (handily also 100g) of boiling water into it.  Jab it with a fork until the stock is dissolved

2. Now put a small sauce pan (like a milk pan) on the scales, zero and put in all the remaining non-liquid ingredients (spread, flour, mustard powder, turmeric, nutritional yeast, garlic powder)

3. Put the pan on a low heat and stir it a bit whilst the spread melts and you get a kind of paste with no lumps

4. When you have a spare moment pop the mug with the stock in it back on the scales, zero it and add anywhere from 150ml to 300ml of soy milk.  Less == thicker, more == thinner.  Personally I just eyeball it and just top up the mug with the soy milk

5. Add 1/3 ish of the stock-milk mixture to the pan

6.  Stir until you get a thinner paste with no massive lumps.  Repeat these steps until you have used all the stock-milk and then leave to thicken as needed.

7.  Optionally add some pepper and maybe some salt depending on what kind of stock cube you used earlier.  The sauce is done at this point so you can taste it an adjust accordingly
